3. **Dehydrated Powders**: DRY, dusty, and matte fine flour texture with visible particle grain. Never wet, creamy, glossy, a ball, or a paste. Clean fresh raw ingredient visible beside or in dedicated gallery hero.
4. **Technical Standards Compliance**:
